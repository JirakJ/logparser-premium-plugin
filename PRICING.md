## 💰 Pricing Tiers

### 🆓 FREE (Community Edition)

**Price:** $0/forever

**Features:**
- ✅ Basic log viewing & syntax highlighting

- ✅ Log level filtering (ERROR, WARN, INFO, DEBUG)
- ✅ Keyword search (simple string matching)
- ✅ Basic timestamp parsing
- ✅ Stack trace folding
- ✅ Export to TXT (max 1,000 lines)

**Limitations:**
- ⚠️ Max 10,000 lines per file
- ⚠️ Max 3 concurrent filters

- ⚠️ No multi-source support
- ⚠️ No correlation tracking

**Target Users:** Individual developers, students, open-source contributors
